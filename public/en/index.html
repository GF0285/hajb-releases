What Hajb does

One extension instead of five: ad blocker, tracker shield, cookie-banner rejecter, pop-up blocker and scam protection.

Ads and sites

  • Ad blocking

    EasyList, AdGuard, uBlock and regional Arabic and Spanish lists, compiled into the browser's own rule engine and refreshed daily, with quick fixes every 6 hours.

  • Anti-adblock stealth

    Hajb's own scriptlets switch off detection code, empty decoy files replace hard blocks, and bait elements keep their size, so sites see no trace of the extension.

  • YouTube

    Removes ad data from the video before it starts, presses Skip when needed, and removes the "ad blockers are not allowed" dialog.

  • Streaming mode

    Strips ad segments from m3u8 and DASH streams, blocks invisible overlays and fake clicks over the player, and closes pop-ups and forced redirects without touching the control bar.

  • Stream links

    Finds the m3u8 or mpd link on the page so you can copy it or open it in VLC or any other player.

  • Download sites

    Skips waiting countdowns and removes the fake buttons around the real download button. It never bypasses captchas or paywalls.

  • Automatic site fix

    If a player or button breaks because of blocking, Hajb relaxes blocking on that site only and remembers it, with an undo button.

  • Live log and element picker

    See every request and the rule applied to it, hide any element with a click, and write your own rules in AdBlock syntax.

Privacy and safety

  • Self-learning engine

    Recognises new ads by size, shape and source, learns from what you hide, and remembers what worked on each site. On your device only.

  • Tracker shield

    Blocks companies that follow you across sites even when no list knows them, strips utm, fbclid and gclid from links, adds noise to your browser fingerprint, sends GPC, and stops WebRTC from leaking your IP.

  • Scam shield

    Warns about phishing pages and look-alike domains, and catches back-button traps, fake virus alerts and the "paste into Win+R" trick.

  • Family controls

    Blocking schedules, a local activity log, and a PIN that protects the settings.

  • Sync

    Carries your settings between your browsers through your own browser account, if you turn it on. PINs and learned data are never synced.

  • Stats

    A local dashboard with ads and trackers blocked, and the time and data you saved.

How we measured, and the limits
  • Headless Chromium from a data-centre IP, one run, all four setups in parallel. Timings compare the setups with each other; they are not absolute numbers.
  • "Requests that got through" are counted with a reference list (Peter Lowe's list plus EasyList and EasyPrivacy domain rules), which slightly favours list-based blockers.
  • 7 sites that showed a captcha or bot wall were left out and never solved, as were sites that failed to load.
  • uBlock Origin Lite used slightly less total browser memory than Hajb (2012 vs 2070 MB). On goal.com and espn.com one or two Google ad requests got through with Hajb, as with uBO Lite; AdGuard blocked them.
  • YouTube changes its system almost every week. This result is a snapshot from test day, not a permanent promise.

Privacy and permissions

Hajb collects no data about you. No accounts, no usage statistics, no servers of its own. The only network traffic it adds is downloading filter lists from their public sources.

  • What the engine learns, the log and family settings stay in browser storage on your device.
  • Sync is optional and goes through your own browser account, without the PIN or learned data.
  • The problem report is saved as a file on your device and contains no addresses of pages you visited.
  • This website has no trackers, no analytics and no third-party fonts.

Install guide

Pick your browser. The screenshots are real, taken from Chrome and Firefox on a desktop; the orange box and number are ours, to show what to click.

Before you start

  1. Download the right file from the top of this page.
  2. For Chromium browsers: unzip the .zip file. You get a folder named hajb.
  3. Move that folder to a stable place such as Documents. If you delete or move it later, the extension stops working.
ChromeEdgeBraveOperaVivaldiFirefoxAndroidUpdate and remove

Chrome

  1. Open the extensions page

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    Switch on "Developer mode" at the top right and leave it on.

  3. Load the folder

    Click "Load unpacked" and choose the hajb folder that contains manifest.json.

  4. Check that it is installed

    A "Hajb 2.0.0" card appears. The blue switch at the bottom means it is on.

  5. Pin it to the toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar, then the pin next to Hajb. Alt+Shift+H hides any element on the page.

Screenshots from Chromium in English. Your extension ID will be different; that is normal.

Edge

  1. Open edge://extensions.
  2. Turn on "Developer mode" in the left panel (at the bottom on desktop).
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the hajb folder.
  4. Make sure the extension is switched on, and pin it from the Extensions button in the toolbar.

The page looks like the Chrome page in the screenshots above, but button positions and labels can differ slightly. We did not capture screenshots for this browser. Chrome

Brave

  1. Open brave://extensions.
  2. Turn on "Developer mode" at the top.
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the hajb folder.
  4. Brave Shields and Hajb can run together. Lower Shields on a site if the two conflict there.

The page looks like the Chrome page in the screenshots above, but button positions and labels can differ slightly. We did not capture screenshots for this browser. Chrome

Opera

  1. Open opera://extensions.
  2. Turn on "Developer mode" at the top of the page.
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the hajb folder.
  4. Turn off Opera's built-in ad blocker in Settings so two blockers don't run at once.

The page looks like the Chrome page in the screenshots above, but button positions and labels can differ slightly. We did not capture screenshots for this browser. Chrome

Vivaldi

  1. Open vivaldi://extensions.
  2. Turn on "Developer mode" at the top.
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the hajb folder.
  4. You can turn off Vivaldi's built-in blocker (Settings > Privacy and Security) to avoid double blocking.

Firefox

The Firefox file is not signed by Mozilla yet. It installs as a temporary add-on, which Firefox removes when it closes, so you load it again each time.

  1. Open the debugging page

    Type about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox in the address bar, or click "This Firefox" in the sidebar.

  2. Load the temporary add-on

    Click "Load Temporary Add-on…" and choose Hajb-2.0.0-firefox.xpi.

  3. Check that it is installed

    Hajb appears under "Temporary Extensions". The yellow warnings are normal: they are Chrome keys that Firefox ignores.

  4. Allow it on all sites

    Open about:addons > Hajb > "Permissions" and turn on "Access your data for all websites", or press "Allow on all sites" in Hajb's settings.

Screenshots from Firefox Nightly in English. Regular Firefox shows the same page; the striped address bar only appears because the browser was under automation while we captured it.

Permanent install without signing (Developer Edition, Nightly or ESR only)

  1. Open about:config and set xpinstall.signatures.required to false.
  2. Open about:addons > gear icon > "Install Add-on From File…" and choose the .xpi file.
  3. Regular Firefox does not allow this. A permanent install there needs Mozilla's signature.

Android

Chrome for Android does not support extensions. Some Chromium-based browsers do, such as Lemur and Quetta. We have not tested Hajb on them yet, so treat this as an experiment.

  1. Install the browser from Google Play.
  2. Download Hajb-2.0.0-chrome.zip from this site.
  3. Open the browser menu > Extensions, and turn on developer mode if asked.
  4. Choose to load from a .zip or .crx file, then pick the Hajb file. Button names differ between browsers.

Firefox for Android only accepts add-ons signed by Mozilla, so Hajb cannot be installed there for now.

Kiwi Browser stopped development in 2025; we don't recommend it.

Update and remove

Update

  1. Download the new version and unzip it.
  2. Replace the contents of the old hajb folder with the new files, in the same place. That keeps your settings.
  3. On the extensions page, click the reload arrow on the Hajb card.
  4. Firefox (temporary): load the new .xpi from about:debugging.

Remove

  1. Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi: click "Remove" on the Hajb card, then delete the folder.
  2. Firefox: the temporary add-on goes away when Firefox closes, or click "Remove" in about:debugging.
  3. Android: from the browser's extensions page.

FAQ and troubleshooting

Is Hajb free?

Yes, completely free, with no ads and no subscription.

Why isn't it in the Chrome or Firefox store?

It hasn't been published to the stores yet, so you install it by hand as the guide shows.

Chrome says "Disable developer mode extensions". What do I do?

Chrome shows this for every hand-installed extension. Close it and leave Developer mode on, or the extension stops.

I get "Manifest file is missing or unreadable".

You picked the wrong folder. Choose the inner hajb folder that has manifest.json directly inside it.

Hajb disappeared from Firefox after a restart.

That is how Firefox treats unsigned add-ons. Load it again from about:debugging, or use Developer Edition for a permanent install.

Firefox says the file is "corrupt" when I open it.

Because the file is unsigned. Install it from about:debugging as the guide shows.

A site broke, or still asks me to disable my ad blocker.

Open the Hajb popup and press "Fix site". If that doesn't help, pause Hajb on that site only and save a "Problem report" from the settings.

Can I use it with another ad blocker?

Better not. Two blockers slow pages down and can break some sites.

Will it always block YouTube ads?

On test day it did, without YouTube noticing. YouTube changes often and Hajb refreshes its rules every 6 hours, but nobody can promise it forever.

Does it bypass age checks or paywalls?

No. Hajb does not bypass age verification, captchas or paywalls.
